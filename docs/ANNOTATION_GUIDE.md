# Semantic annotation guide

Annotate a candidate from the original source window, not the model output. Record start/end milliseconds, actor and target hypotheses, the decisive timestamps, label, outcome, ambiguity flag and reviewer. Keep train/validation separate from a frozen milestone test set.

When intent is not visible, annotate `unknown` and explain the competing labels. A clearance is a safety-first disposal under pressure; an interception is the opponent's deliberate disruption of a pass; a recovery is control of a free ball; a duel or tackle requires a contested challenge. Do not use projected pitch coordinates of an airborne ball as decisive evidence.
